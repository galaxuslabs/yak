`@yak/solid` now needs `solid-js` and `@solidjs/web` 2.0.0 or newer and `@solidjs/vite-plugin` 3.0.0 or newer, upgrade from the release candidates with `npm i solid-js@2 @solidjs/web@2` and `npm i -D @solidjs/vite-plugin@3`

`@yak/solid` is no longer experimental and now shares its version number with `@yak/react`, so it jumps from 0.5.0 to the `@yak/react` version of this release
